TERRA PAVILION

Location

Ahmedabad

Year

2024

Typology

Residential

Area

000 SQ FT

Clients

Sameer & Hemangini Sinha

The Terra Pavilion is a weekend retreat designed for a couple with a passion for art, culinary experiences, and socializing with friends. Situated adjacent to a golf course, the pavilion provides a versatile space for relaxation, entertainment, and rejuvenation. The design allows the pavilion to remain open during favorable seasons, while sliding doors enable it to be enclosed during the monsoon or summer months. The interior can be used as a single unified space or segmented into distinct areas, offering flexibility for various activities. A central structural feature houses a suspended spiral staircase, which serves as a sculptural focal point, leading to the lower level— referred to as the “earth level.” This level contains the bedrooms and a private family lounge, all of which open into sunken courtyards. The solid, grounded nature of the earth level contrasts with the lightness of the pavilion above, enhancing the overall architectural experience.
